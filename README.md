## What the calendar actually is this year

Charlotte Tilbury swapped the gold finish for a festive red chest covered in constellation print, with crystal star handles on each of the 12 pull-out drawers. The whole thing is built to survive past December — the drawers line up as a jewellery box or makeup organiser once the products are out. If you've bought one of these before, you know the packaging is half the reason people pay for it.

Inside, the contents run 15 items deep: makeup and skincare across the brand's bestsellers, plus — for the first time — two accessories. One is a limited-edition white quartz gua sha inspired by the tools used at the brand's Covent Garden skin spa. The other is a mini Pillow Talk charm makeup bag that's exclusive to the calendar.

Worth flagging early: the drawers aren't numbered. Last year's chest wasn't either. You can open them in any order you like, which is either charming or ruins the whole point, depending on how seriously you take advent.

The UK price dropped from £175 in 2025 while the Australian price fell from AU$342. The US price stayed at $220.

That's a genuine improvement on paper, but the value claims deserve a second look. Value figures assume you'd otherwise pay full retail for every item, including travel sizes you might never buy separately. The Independent's review made that point directly: this year's calendar offers roughly a 50% saving, while some competitor calendars get closer to 80%. It also flagged that Charlotte Tilbury skipped complexion products entirely, which is odd for a brand whose concealer and loose powder are among its newer hits.

So: good value, not the best value on the shelf. Whether that matters depends on whether you'd actually use what's inside — and if you already own a full-size Beauty Light Wand and a tube of Magic Cream, part of this calendar is just a restock.

## Is the Charlotte Tilbury advent calendar worth it?

Three or four full-size products plus eleven or twelve travel sizes and two accessories, in a box you'll keep, for £170. If you're a Charlotte Tilbury regular, that's close to a year of restocking in one purchase — the 2025 coverage made the same argument and it holds up here.

If you're not, the maths is less clean. Travel sizes are where the value claim gets soft. A 15ml Magic Cream is a nice way to test the formula, not a replacement for the full jar. And a gua sha is a gua sha; the fact that this one is white quartz with a Charlotte Tilbury stamp on it doesn't change how it works.

Where it makes the most sense:

- You already use Pillow Talk shades and want the full-size versions at less than retail.
- You want to try the skincare line without committing to full sizes of Magic Cream, Magic Serum and the cleanser.
- You need a gift for someone who's into beauty and will notice the packaging.

Where it doesn't:

- You want mostly full-size makeup for your money. Look at calendars with a higher full-size ratio.
- You want complexion products. There aren't any this year.
